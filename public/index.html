Books, Out Loud

Live, immersive performances of great Christian literature

A literary salon with moral imagination, wit, mystery, and grace.

From Chesterton and Lewis to Flannery O’Connor and Fulton Sheen, BooksOutLoud brings classic Christian writing off the page and into the room—performed with dramatic clarity, elegant restraint, and a distinctly old-world literary atmosphere. Every program is presented by Jeromy Darling, an award-winning actor, playwright, and solo performer.
