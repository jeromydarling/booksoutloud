Programs built for live rooms

Each performance is shaped as an intimate literary event: one voice, one text, and an audience drawn into wit, mystery, beauty, and spiritual gravity. Most programs land between forty-two and fifty-five minutes of performed text, with natural room for a Q&A afterward.
